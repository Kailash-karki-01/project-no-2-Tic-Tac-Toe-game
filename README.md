# 🎮 Tic Tac Toe Game

A simple and interactive **Tic Tac Toe game** built with two different game modes: Player vs Computer and Two Player Mode. Players can enjoy the classic Tic Tac Toe experience, track their scores, and compete to win.

## ✨ Features

* **🤖 Player vs Computer:** Play against the computer and try to outsmart it.
* **👥 Two Player Mode:** Play against a friend on the same device.
* **🏆 Scoreboard:** Keeps track of the scores and awards a point to the winner after each game.
* **🎉 Winning Message:** Displays a message announcing the winner at the end of each round.
* **🔄 Multiple Game Modes:** Switch between playing against the computer and competing with another player.

## 🎯 How to Play

1. Choose your preferred game mode.
2. Take turns placing your mark (X or O) on the game board.
3. Be the first to get three marks in a row, column, or diagonal to win.
4. The winner receives a point on the scoreboard.
5. Keep playing and try to achieve the highest score!
